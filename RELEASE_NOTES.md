# VoiceType 1.1.0: The AI Update ✨

VoiceType just got a major intelligence upgrade. We've integrated Google's **Gemini Flash** model directly into the widget to help you write better, faster.

## 🌟 What's New

### Smart Rewrite
Don't just dictate—prefect it.
*   **One-Click Polish**: After speaking, click the blue ✨ **Sparkles** icon on the widget.
*   **Instant Grammar Fixes**: The AI automatically corrects typos, punctuation, and sentence structure.
*   **Streaming Output**: Watch your rewritten text appear in real-time.

### Refined Experience
*   **Smart Selection**: The app now intelligently selects only the text it just typed before replacing it, ensuring the rest of your document stays safe.
*   **Design System**: A fresh coat of paint with our new **Pro Blue** theme across the App and Widget.
*   **Performance**: Faster startup and smoother widget animations.

## 🛠 Fixes
*   Fixed an issue where "Replace" would select all text in the field.
*   Fixed "Thinking" tokens sometimes appearing in the output.
*   Resolved keychain prompting issues by moving to standard storage for API keys.

## How to Install
1.  Download `VoiceType.dmg`
2.  Drag to Applications
3.  Open and grant permissions
4.  Add your Gemini API Key in Settings (Free)
