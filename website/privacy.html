Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

TL;DR: VoiceType processes dictation locally. If you use AI Rewrite, text is sent to Google Gemini. We don't collect, store, or transmit your data to our own servers.

What we collect

Nothing. VoiceType does not collect any personal information, usage data, or analytics. There are no accounts, no telemetry, and no network requests to our servers.

How speech recognition works

VoiceType uses WhisperKit, an on-device AI model, to convert your voice to text. This means:

  • All audio processing happens entirely on your Mac
  • Your voice recordings are never sent to any server
  • Transcriptions are never stored or logged

AI Rewrite (Optional)

If you use the Smart Rewrite feature (Sparkles icon), the selected text is sent to Google's Gemini API for processing. This data is processed by Google to provide the rewritten text and is subject to their Terms of Service. VoiceType does not store or log this data.

Permissions we require

VoiceType needs permissions to function:

  • Microphone Access: To hear your voice for transcription.
  • Accessibility Access: To insert text at your cursor position.

Network usage

VoiceType requires network only for:

  • Downloading the Whisper AI model on first launch (~150MB).
  • Sending text to Google Gemini (only when you click Smart Rewrite).

Data storage

VoiceType stores only your preferences (like model selection) locally using macOS's standard UserDefaults. No audio, transcriptions, or personal data is ever stored.

Third-party services

VoiceType uses Google Gemini API for the optional Rewrite feature. No other third-party analytics or trackers are used.
